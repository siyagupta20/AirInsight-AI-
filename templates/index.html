How AirInsight AI Discovers Hidden Pollution Dynamics.

By extracting high-dimensional chemical concentrations without human label bias, our unsupervised pipeline automatically detects natural groupings, dense pollution pockets, and anomalous outlier events.

🧹

1. Preprocessing & Scaling

Handled missing values using median imputation, selected essential features (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3), and standardized dimensions with StandardScaler.

🌳

2. Hierarchical Clustering

Applied Agglomerative Clustering with Ward's minimum variance linkage to construct dendrogram clusters of similar air quality regimes.

🔍

3. DBSCAN Hotspot Detection

Density-Based Spatial Clustering discovers arbitrarily shaped dense pollution cores while isolating hazardous outlier events as noise (label -1).

📊

4. PCA Dimensionality Reduction

Synthesized 10+ correlated pollutant vectors into 2 Principal Components (PC1 & PC2) for intuitive 2D scatter pattern visualization.

📐

5. Mathematical Evaluation

Evaluated cluster separability and cohesion using Silhouette Score, Davies-Bouldin Index, and Calinski-Harabasz Score.

🏥

6. Clinical Health Advisory

Automated clinical recommendation engine converting calculated sub-indices into tailored guidance for sensitive groups, masks, and purifiers.
